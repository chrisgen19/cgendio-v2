## Built

A WordPress front end for marketing, a custom Laravel learning management system for the courses, and Shopify for registration and payment.

## Hard part

Joining the three into one journey, from sign-up to certificate, with course rules that change by state.

## What's in it

- WordPress pages for course information and marketing
- Custom Laravel LMS that tracks each student's progress
- Shopify for enrolment and payment
- Course rules and content that change by state
- Certificates issued when a course is finished
